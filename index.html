About LIRAH

Local expertise. Broad engineering capability.

LIRAH Engineering Co., Ltd. is a Cambodian construction and engineering company established in 2012 by engineers with local and overseas experience.

Our work spans civil construction, electrical infrastructure, buildings, roads, canals, riverbank protection and MEP works.
